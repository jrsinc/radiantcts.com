Who we are

Radiant Tech & Consulting (“Radiant,” “we,” “us”) provides technology, business, compliance and governance, financial and healthcare consulting services. This policy explains how we handle personal information collected through this website and in the course of our business. Questions about this policy can be sent to contact@radiantcts.com.

Information we collect

We collect only the information we need:

  • Information you send us. When you contact us through the website form or by email, we receive the details you choose to share, such as your name, work email, organization, area of interest and message.
  • Client engagement information. When we provide services, we may receive business contact details and information needed to perform the engagement, under the terms of our agreement with your organization.
  • Technical information. Our hosting provider may automatically record basic technical data, such as IP address, browser type and pages visited, to operate and secure the website.

This website does not use advertising cookies or tracking pixels.

How we use information

  • To respond to inquiries and arrange consultations
  • To deliver, manage and invoice our services
  • To keep our website and systems secure
  • To meet our legal, regulatory and professional obligations

We do not sell personal information, and we do not use it for purposes unrelated to the reason it was provided.

How we share information

We share personal information only when necessary, with:

  • Service providers who support our operations, such as website hosting, email and document storage, under confidentiality obligations
  • Professional advisers, such as lawyers, accountants and insurers
  • Authorities, when required by law or to protect our legal rights

Our website loads fonts from Google Fonts, which means your browser connects to Google’s servers when you visit. Google’s handling of that data is covered by its own privacy policy.

Health and sensitive information

Our website is not designed to receive health information, and we ask that you do not send protected health information or other sensitive personal data through the contact form or by unencrypted email. Where a healthcare engagement requires access to protected health information, we handle it only under a written agreement with the client, such as a business associate agreement, and in line with applicable law, including HIPAA where it applies.

Security and retention

We use reasonable administrative, technical and physical safeguards to protect personal information, and we limit access to people who need it for their work. We keep information only as long as needed for the purposes described in this policy or as required by law or our client agreements, then delete or anonymize it securely.

Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or receive a copy of your personal information, or to object to certain uses. To make a request, email contact@radiantcts.com. We will respond within the time required by applicable law and may need to verify your identity first.

Children

Our website and services are intended for businesses and professionals. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The “Last reviewed” date at the top of this page shows when it was last changed. Significant changes will be highlighted on this page.

Contact us

For any privacy question or request, contact us at contact@radiantcts.com.
